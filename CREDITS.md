## Characters, guns, shoot effects

Pixel art from the Craftpix free cyberpunk pack. Terms: [Craftpix file licenses](https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/).

This repository ships **only the runtime frames the game loads**, not the original zip/PSD packs:

- `assets/craftpix/characters/cyborg/` — Player 1
- `assets/craftpix/characters/biker/` — Player 2
- `assets/craftpix/guns/` — pistol overlays
- `assets/craftpix/shoot_effects/` — muzzle flash

Do not copy those folders out as a standalone asset pack.
